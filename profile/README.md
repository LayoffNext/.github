# LayoffNext

LayoffNext is an employee-first career transition and financial wellness platform that helps professionals prepare for, navigate, and recover from layoffs and career changes.

## What We Provide

- Layoff planning checklists
- Severance guidance
- Unemployment benefit resources
- COBRA and health insurance planning
- Emergency budgeting tools
- Resume and job search resources
- Career transition guides
- Financial planning resources

## Website

🌐 https://www.layoffnext.com

## Mission

Our mission is to help employees make informed financial and career decisions during periods of uncertainty through practical guidance, educational resources, and interactive planning tools.
